Design thesisThe overview explains the bill as a mechanism: workload × model price × cache behavior. Essential findings no longer hide behind measurement tabs. SignatureThe aligned agent-cost trace behaves like a profiler: spend, processed input, uncached input, and session activity share one temporal field. Comparison rule7D, 30D, and 90D compare with the immediately preceding equal interval. ALL has no automatic comparison. Totals, rates, and efficiency are distinguished. Honesty markAll values in this study are illustrative. The cache-benefit estimate requires provider-correct pricing and should remain explicitly labeled as an estimate.
